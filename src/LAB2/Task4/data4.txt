A hidden pond, shaded by overhanging trees, is surrounded by dense reeds and wildflowers. In the water, koi fish with vibrant scales swim gracefully in gentle circles, occasionally surfacing to catch a fly or two. A family of frogs rests on floating leaves, watching as tiny insects skim across the water. Dragonflies, with wings glinting in the sunlight, dart playfully above the pond, casting fleeting reflections on the water’s surface.
